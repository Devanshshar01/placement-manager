# User Guide

## For Students

### Getting Started
1.  **Register**: Go to the registration page, select "Student", and fill in your details including Roll Number and CGPA.
2.  **Login**: Use your email and password to log in.

### Managing Your Profile
1.  Navigate to the **Profile** section.
2.  Add your skills (e.g., Java, Python) by typing and pressing Enter.
3.  Add links to your Resume, LinkedIn, and Portfolio.
4.  Click **Save Changes** to update your profile.

### Applying for Jobs
1.  Go to the **Drives** section to see a list of available job openings.
2.  Click **View Details** on a drive to see the job description and eligibility criteria.
3.  If you are eligible, click the **Apply Now** button.
4.  You can track your applications in the **My Applications** section.

### Using the AI Chatbot
1.  Click the chat icon in the bottom right corner.
2.  Ask questions like "Am I eligible for Google?" or "How can I improve my resume?".
3.  The AI assistant will provide personalized answers based on your profile.

## For Administrators

### Dashboard Overview
- View key metrics like Total Students, Placed Students, and Active Drives.
- See a list of recent activities and quick actions.

### Managing Companies & Drives
1.  **Add Company**: Go to the **Companies** section and click "Add Company".
2.  **Create Drive**: Once a company is added, go to **Drives** and click "Create Drive". Select the company and fill in job details.

### Managing Applications
1.  Go to the **Applications** section.
2.  Use the filters on the left to find specific applications (e.g., by Company or Status).
3.  **View Details**: Click on an application to see the student's profile and skills match.
4.  **Update Status**: You can Shortlist, Select, or Reject candidates individually or in bulk using the checkboxes.

### Analytics
- Check the **Analytics** section for visual charts on placement trends and branch-wise performance.
